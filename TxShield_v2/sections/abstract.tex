\begin{abstract}
\RevBlock{Decentralized Finance (DeFi) protocols have become prime targets for sophisticated attacks, with losses exceeding \$3.4 billion in 2025. However, existing detection approaches face fundamental limitations. Traditional rule-based detection tools are limited in their ability to capture diverse attack behaviors at runtime, while advanced large language models (LLMs) exhibit limitations due to insufficient task-specific knowledge and hallucinations. To this end, we observe that DeFi attacks, despite diverse implementations, exhibit recurring behavioral patterns at the semantic level, suggesting that their apparent diversity masks a small number of underlying semantic structures. In this paper, we present \approach, a Retrieval-Augmented Generation framework for transaction-level DeFi attack detection. \approach constructs a knowledge base of historical attacks with LLM-generated behavioral summaries, retrieves historical cases and applies similarity filtering to select relevant historical context, and selects prompts according to the available context to guide LLM reasoning by analogy with retrieved patterns. XGBoost estimates transaction risk to control the use of retrieved cases and provides an auxiliary probability hint for LLM reasoning. The LLM produces the final classification.}

\RevBlock{On an evaluation set constructed from 364 DeFiHackLabs attack transactions and 364 benign transactions, \approach{} achieves 92.93\% F1 and 97.87\% precision. Compared with direct XGBoost classification, it improves F1 by 6.45 percentage points and reduces the number of false-positive transactions from 60 to 7.}
\end{abstract}
